Noise Generator

Explore different types of noise! White noise has equal energy across all frequencies, pink noise has equal energy per octave (1/f spectrum), and brown noise has even more low-frequency emphasis (1/f² spectrum).

White Noise

Equal power across all frequencies. Sounds like static or a TV hiss.

Pink Noise

Equal power per octave. Sounds like rainfall or rustling leaves.

Brown Noise

More low-frequency power. Sounds like a deep rumble or waterfall.
